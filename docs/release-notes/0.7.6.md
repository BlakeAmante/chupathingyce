## What's new in 0.7.6 (Unreleased)

**Changes**
- Chupathingy keeps its own saves on Windows and Linux; your profiles and game types are copied over the first time.
- Online Games looks like the Xbox's menus: their fonts, glow and panels, larger rows, the Xbox's buttons for a controller (key caps for the keyboard), and a tag on every game's map for its family (XBX, HCE, HMD, GBX). A game with a password shows a lock, and the ping column shows the round trip to a host you are connected to.
- The Halo PC menus are called Gearbox now: `display.menus = "gearbox"` (`"pc"` still works).
- Discord now shows Chupathingy for players whose settings still named the old application.

**Fixes**
- When a Custom Edition map is too big for the tag cache, debug.txt now says plainly how much room it needs and how much there is.
- A Custom Edition map the game can't play now says why on screen (for example, that Custom Edition's bitmaps.map is missing from maps_ce, or that the map is too big for the tag cache), in place of the damaged disc message.
